
# 🥤 Vending Machine Sales & Stock Optimization Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze vending machine sales performance, payment behavior, product demand, and inventory requirements, enabling data-driven decisions for sales and stock optimization.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Vending Machine Sales & Stock Optimization Dashboard** provides a comprehensive analysis of vending machine transaction data by tracking product sales, sales deviations, payment behavior, machine performance, product demand, and inventory requirements.

The dashboard enables users to compare sales across different periods, analyze customer payment behavior, identify high-demand products, and support stock optimization decisions through interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall vending machine sales performance.
- Compare sales performance between different months.
- Identify products with significant sales deviations.
- Analyze customer payment behavior.
- Compare cash and non-cash transactions.
- Evaluate product demand across vending machines.
- Identify peak-demand products.
- Analyze demand variability.
- Support inventory and stock optimization decisions.

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Deviation Analysis

The **Deviation Analysis** page compares sales performance between two selected months.

Users can analyze:

- Sales for Month 1
- Sales for Month 2
- Sales Deviation
- Sales Deviation %
- Product-level performance
- Category-level performance
- Machine-level performance

Interactive month selectors allow users to compare different periods and identify products or machines experiencing significant changes in sales.

---

### 2. Payment Behavior Analysis

The **Payment Behavior Analysis** page focuses on customer payment patterns.

The dashboard analyzes:

- Cash transactions
- Non-cash transactions
- Payment methods
- Payment trends
- Machine-level payment behavior
- Category-level payment behavior

This helps identify how customers interact with different payment methods across vending machines.

---

### 3. Stock Optimization

The **Stock Optimization** page focuses on product demand and inventory requirements.

The analysis includes:

- Average monthly quantity
- Peak monthly quantity
- Monthly quantity standard deviation
- Product demand
- Category-level demand
- Machine-level demand
- Stock recommendations

The dashboard uses demand patterns to support stock-related decisions such as:

- Increase Stock
- Maintain Stock
- Reduce Stock
